Hunting for suspicious things (Network)
New listening servers	
grep LISTEN i_netstat.txt

Active connections	
grep ESTABLISHED i_netstat.txt

Anything not local
grep -vE "127\.0\.0\.1|0\.0\.0\.0|UNIX" i_netstat.txt


Odd port numbers
grep -E ":4444|:1337|:31337|:6666" i_netstat.txt

Sniffing	
grep -i promisc i_ifconfig.txt

Odd raw sockets	
cat i_raw.txt
