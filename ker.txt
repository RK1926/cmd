Step 1. 
Loaded modules (linux_lsmod)
diff <(awk '{print $2,$3}' c_lsmod.txt | sort) <(awk '{print $2,$3}' i_lsmod.txt | sort)

Step 2. 
Kernel messages (linux_dmesg)
diff <(sed 's/^\[[^]]*\] //' c_dmesg.txt) <(sed 's/^\[[^]]*\] //' i_dmesg.txt)
grep -iE "taint|verification|module|vbox|lime" i_dmesg.txt

Step 3. Hidden modules (linux_check_modules and linux_hidden_modules)
cat i_checkmod.txt
cat i_hidden.txt

Step 4. Dump a module to compare (linux_moddump)
mkdir -p ~/out/c_mods ~/out/i_mods
volatility -f $C --profile=$P linux_moddump -D ~/out/c_mods -r e1000
volatility -f $I --profile=$P linux_moddump -D ~/out/i_mods -r e1000
ls -l ~/out/c_mods ~/out/i_mods
md5sum ~/out/c_mods/* ~/out/i_mods/*
